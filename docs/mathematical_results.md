## Template for a new entry

1. **ID and title:** a stable, citable identifier.
2. **Provenance:** author, date, file, section or external reference.
3. **Notation:** relationship to the `(target, source)` basis and time indices.
4. **Hypotheses:** graph class, scattering matrices, initial states,
   measurement instrument and required nonzero conditions.
5. **Statement:** full theorem, including quantifiers and exceptional cases.
6. **Proof:** proof text or a verifiable reference; mark any remaining gaps.
7. **Status:** proposed / proof checked / numerically checked / integrated.
8. **Simulation consequences:** allowed reduction, cost and validity domain.
9. **Validation:** exact small examples, randomized cross-checks, boundary
   cases and statistical error bounds.
10. **Implementation:** strategy, tests, version and known limitations.
